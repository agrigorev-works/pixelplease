Pixel Merriweather

Merriweather is the default bundled demo font in pixelplease. Its sturdy serif shapes stay readable after the pixel grid is applied, making it a useful starting point for posters, editorial mockups, and retro interface experiments.

Merriweather becomes a generated pixel-style TTF.

Why start with Merriweather?

  • It has enough stroke contrast to feel editorial after pixelization.
  • Its larger forms survive coarse pixel grids better than delicate scripts.
  • It is bundled with source OFL license material for demo exports.

Try it

Open pixelplease and select Merriweather.
